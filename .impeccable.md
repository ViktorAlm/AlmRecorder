## Design Context

### Users
Privacy-conscious macOS power users who record meetings, calls, lectures, and voice memos and
want them transcribed, searched, and organized **entirely locally** — nothing leaves the Mac.
They value trust, control, and craft, and many are Nordic/Swedish (the app ships Swedish KB-Whisper
models; "Alm" is a Swedish surname).

### Brand Personality
Refined, calm, trustworthy. Three words: **understated · premium · Scandinavian.** The product
should feel like a well-made tool you'd trust with private material — quiet confidence, never loud,
neon, or flashy-techy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Apple / Things-grade restraint: generous negative space, precise geometry, soft depth, understated
motion. Palette drawn from **Swedish kitchen-cabinet colors** — muted and natural, matte not glossy:

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Sage green (primary) | cabinet sage | `#7C8A6B` |
| Dusty blue (alt primary) | slate blue | `#8AA1AD` |
| Warm cream (light surface / ink-on-dark) | linseed white | `#F4EFE3` |
| Soft charcoal (ink, never pure black) | warm charcoal | `#2E2D29` |
| Brass / oak (single warm accent, sparingly) | brass | `#B5894E` |
| Putty / greige (neutral) | greige | `#CFC8B8` |

**Anti-references:** the generic "AI" look (purple→blue gradients, neon-on-black, glassmorphism
everywhere), pure `#000`/`#fff`, loud saturated color, monospace-as-decoration, big rounded icons
above every heading.

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet confidence** — restraint over decoration; space and proportion carry the design.
2. **Natural, muted color** — Scandinavian kitchen tones; tint neutrals warm; one accent, used sparingly.
3. **Precise geometry** — clean, intentional shapes; nothing arbitrary.
4. **Soft depth, not flat** — gentle layering like Things; no hard drop-shadows or heavy glass.
5. **Trust through craft** — every detail signals "private, local, well-made."
